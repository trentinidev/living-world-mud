# 0016 — Paid hammer training and tool wear

## Decision

The `apprenticeship` scenario carries forward the funded orders and adds an optional lesson. Instruction uses the shared forge slot and requires Ada to be present and idle. The timed action reserves tuition and occupies Ada as well as the student. The recipe unlocks at completion.

A renter who knows the recipe can craft a hammer from one bar of iron and one firewood bundle. The hammer receives the same maker and material records as a hoe. A smith carrying one gets a quality bonus based on its quality score. One of six uses is spent per completed forging action, with a recorded inventory sink after the last use.

## Consequences

Players can spend time and money to unlock a useful tool. Practice still grants blacksmithing experience when a piece is finished. The audit verifies the physical stock of both tools against their item records.
